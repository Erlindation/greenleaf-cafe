Why We Serve Natural, Unroasted Coffee

At Greenleaf Café, we take pride in offering natural, unroasted coffee beans—known as green coffee— because we believe in preserving the purest form of flavor and nutrients. This choice reflects our commitment to sustainability and health. Alongside our ecological menu and 100% vegan pastries, we strive to reduce our carbon footprint while creating a cozy, conscious space for our community.
